Partner Incubation Station: three directions

Same content and the same four areas, with the station idea used at different strengths. Open each one, then pick a direction or mix parts.

An interactive map of four lines with shared stops. Selecting a line highlights its route. The assistant is "the desk".

 B. Departure boardTransit in one bold element
