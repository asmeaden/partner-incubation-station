Advisory is where most partners start. FinOps for AI and agent adoption run across every engagement once customers are spending on credits and putting agents into real processes.

AI advisory practice

A Copilot Cowork engagement runs in four steps over six weeks, led by cohort leaders and enabled by IT for cost governance.

Get the Proof of Value Kit
  1. Weeks 0 to 1AssessAgree objectives, cohorts, success metrics and the credit budget before any task runs.
  2. Weeks 1 to 2IdentifyTurn the cohort's day-to-day work into a short, measurable, costed task list.
  3. Weeks 3 to 4Implement and proveSet policies and limits, run the tasks live and capture before-and-after evidence.
  4. Weeks 4 to 6ScaleDeliver the value and cost readout, then plan the next cohorts and governance.

Agent adoption and change

Agents change business processes, not just personal habits. Lead the people side of the change through five phases.

Playbook resources
  1. PlanAlign the agent to business priorities, set success measures and baseline them.
  2. ImplementPilot end to end, launch comms and training, run hypercare with a fallback.
  3. AdoptLeaders and managers reinforce the new way of working; act on feedback.
  4. ManageProve value against the baseline and keep governance and performance in check.
  5. ImproveMove from project to operations, then scale to the next process.
